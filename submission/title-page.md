## 1. Recommended title

> **From Loss Accounting to Feedback Loops: A Coupling Framework for Electrochemical Aging in Lithium-Based Batteries**

**Character count:** 113 (including spaces and colon). **Word count:** 15. Within any plausible journal title limit.

**Why this title.** It names both contributions in the order the paper delivers them — the ledger first, the loop topology second — and the phrase "A Coupling Framework" signals a *Perspective* (an argument with a position) rather than a Review (a survey). It contains the two phrases a reader would search for ("loss accounting", "feedback loops") and the scope qualifier ("lithium-based") that keeps the paper honestly inside the journal's Li-battery center of gravity without forcing a supercapacitor angle.

**Decision — RESOLVED 2026-11-04 by the Lead: AMERICAN ENGLISH. The title stays "Aging".** The package is *not* to be flipped to British. The Lead's rationale, recorded so it can be applied consistently: the journal accepts either variety but demands consistency; our keyword list is American; the majority of the cited literature uses American forms; and the manuscript and its figure legends have already been drafted in American English ("stabilization", "optimization", "catalyze"). ## 6. Abstract (for the title page / submission system field)

**Count check — two defects in the dossier draft, both fixed below.** The dossier's abstract draft (`06-proposal/outline.md` §2) reads on paper as "198 words" but actually runs **210 words** by whitespace tokenisation, which **exceeds the ≤200-word limit**. **Compliant abstract — 191 words** (the text now in `manuscript.md`; count it there, not here — this copy is for the submission-system field and the two must be kept identical):

> Capacity fade in lithium-based batteries is reported mechanism by mechanism: an interphase study quotes Coulombic efficiency, a fracture study crack density, a cathode study parts-per-million dissolved manganese. Each is rigorous, yet the three cannot be combined into one account of why a given cell failed at a stated cycle. The mechanisms are not additive: dissolution and fracture create fresh surface, feeding interphase growth and plating, which raise the overpotential that drives both. Four feedback loops therefore convert linear fade into a sharp knee, and since each closes on a comparison between an accumulated quantity and an available margin, the knee is a design variable. All capacity loss passes through three channels — loss of lithium inventory (LLI), loss of active material (LAM), kinetic or polarization loss (KL) — and each mechanism leaves a characteristic trajectory, not a magnitude. Applied to 24 published cells, the prescription returns a positive coupling strength in 22, and the knee moves 2-fold for a 1.3-fold change in the operating margin against a 400-fold change in loop gain. We state six falsifiable predictions, a decision tree that tests whether coupling is present, and a minimum reporting set.
